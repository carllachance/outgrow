What Outgrow is

What do you want to outgrow?

Outgrow is a goal-first support product for real-life change. It helps people move away from patterns that are no longer working and toward a version of success they define for themselves. It offers gentle structure, practical support, and room to revise as life changes.

User-defined success Support that fits real life Calm, transparent guidance

What do you want to outgrow?

Start with the person’s own words, not a preset model.

The first step is intentionally broad. A goal can be small, specific, messy, or still forming. Outgrow helps clarify the direction and build one honest next step without forcing a perfect plan on day one.

How support works

Build a realistic framework, then revisit and revise.

Outgrow is not a strict tracker and not a generic coach. It is structured support that stays grounded in the user’s own definition of success.

Goal-first onboarding

Outgrow can ask a light clarification question when intent is ambiguous, then suggest practical areas to support.

Daily follow-through

The day view keeps support grounded: one clear next step, contextual nudges, and low-pressure momentum.

Food as one practical pathway

Meal support is strong and visible, but not the whole identity.

Meal planning, meal logging, recipes, and shopping-oriented structure are major value today. Outgrow keeps that strong food pathway visible while also supporting routines, movement, sleep rhythm, reminders, and day structure when those are part of the same goal.

Flexible frameworks for real life

Start incomplete. Add structure when it helps.

Framework-building is optional and intentionally minimal at first. Users can return to their goal and update wording, scope, and support over time instead of being locked into rigid assumptions.

Transparent support, not pushy automation

Clear support that does not push.

Outgrow is built to be understandable, not pushy. It favors clear suggestions over opaque automation, appears when context is strong enough to help, and stays quiet when it is not. Human judgment and revision are part of the value, not a fallback.

What exists now / where it is heading

Early product, serious direction.

Today, this repo contains an MVP with onboarding, daily support surfaces, meal flows, journal/reflection tools, and safety foundations. The direction is a broader growth support product built for how change actually happens: gradual, revisable, and personal.

Current status: working MVP in active refinement. Food support is a major live pathway; broader cross-domain support is in progress.
Outgrow is being built as a real product — calm, transparent, and shaped in public.
