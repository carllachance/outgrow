# Outgrow Safety Policy

Outgrow supports gentle, sustainable habit change. It must not become a tool for shame, coercion, compulsive self-surveillance, unhealthy dependence, social comparison spirals, or harmful health guidance.

## Core principles
- Support without pressure.
- Trends, not verdicts.
- Experiments, not prescriptions.
- Calm over compulsion.
- User sovereignty with product boundaries.
- When risk rises, freedom narrows.

## Non-negotiable boundary
Outgrow must not allow behavior, content, or AI output to reliably turn the product into a tool for self-punishment, body shame, restriction, compensation, compulsive tracking, dependency, humiliation, harmful comparison, manipulative self-talk, pseudo-medical certainty, or harmful reinforcement.

## Risk tiers and response
- **Tier 0 (ordinary support):** gentle routines, reflection, low-risk journaling.
- **Tier 1 (caution):** soften tone, reduce precision, avoid escalation.
- **Tier 2 (elevated concern):** pause optimization framing, bounded support only, suggest stepping back.
- **Tier 3 (strong red flags):** stop tracking, hide historic progress, suspend enabling features, switch to constrained safety mode.

## Red-flag intervention
Strong red flags immediately trigger safety mode. While active:
- Tracking is stopped.
- Historic progress remains stored but hidden.
- Progress views, streaks, trends, action plans, adaptive coaching, and community posting are suspended.

## Reset and prolonged safe mode
Reset is available in **Profile only** and is deliberate and bounded. Repeated red-flag cycling or reset-loop behavior can trigger **prolonged safe mode** where restrictions remain active longer.

## Allowed behavior in safety mode
Only low-risk surfaces remain:
- Supportive language.
- Non-optimizing journaling.
- Neutral profile/settings access.
- Reset status visibility.
- Human-support redirection when appropriate.

## AI guardrails
The AI must never shame, prescribe restriction/compensation, moralize food/rest/movement, diagnose from wearable signals, provide treatment advice, encourage dependency, or intensify goals during distress.

The AI may reflect, offer a few low-risk options, suggest gentle experiments, explain uncertainty, and decline unsafe requests.
