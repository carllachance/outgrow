# Outgrow Doctrine

## Core promise

Outgrow is a privacy-first adaptive nutrition support product built on a quiet promise:

**I’m here to help you need me less.**

It is not designed to gamify health, moralize food, or keep users dependent on constant tracking. The product should help users build judgment, steadier routines, and a way of eating that fits their real life.

## What Outgrow is

- Calm, grounded support for behavior change.
- A product that treats real life as the default, not an interruption.
- A system that supports both a long horizon and a realistic current week.
- A place where showing up matters even when the pace is uneven.
- A product that tapers support as confidence and judgment grow.

## What Outgrow is not

- A streak machine.
- A shame loop.
- A food-morality engine.
- A trophy cabinet.
- A substitute therapist.
- A product that mistakes intensity for progress.

## Emotional stance

Outgrow should feel like the best kind of running culture:

- no one is grading your pace
- people are glad you’re here
- encouragement exists without comparison
- showing up counts
- slower progress is still progress

## Tone rules

Outgrow should sound:

- calm
- respectful
- practical
- observant
- lightly encouraging
- specific when possible

Outgrow should not sound:

- hypey
- moralizing
- parental
- surveillant
- corporate-coach-like
- clinically cold

## Core phrases

These are close to the center of the brand and should be used intentionally:

- The fact that you’re here still matters.
- Showing up counts.
- You’re still moving.
- Better to keep it workable than make it stricter.
- Training wheels are coming off, but I’m still here.
- You do not need to undo anything.
- The pace can change without the progress disappearing.

## Product structure

Outgrow should always preserve three layers:

### Long horizon
**I know I’ve Outgrown this when…**

### Current week
**This week, success looks like…**

### Active workstream
**Current focus**

These layers should remain distinct. The long horizon is not a weekly contract. The weekly lens is not a moral score. The current focus is not an identity label.

## Product non-negotiables

- Praise must never create pressure.
- Privacy is structural, not decorative.
- The app must be useful with minimal input.
- Support intensity must map to real product behavior.
- Chef suggestions must optimize for repeat-worthiness, not novelty.
- Hard weeks must preserve dignity.
- Real progress may be slower than originally hoped.
- The app should help users adjust expectations without framing drift as failure.

## Final summary

Outgrow should help people move from chaos to judgment, from shame to steadiness, and from dependence to quiet independence — without treating a hard week like a personal failure.
