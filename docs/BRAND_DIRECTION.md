# Outgrow Brand Direction

## Brand feeling

Outgrow should feel calm, reflective, private, and quietly encouraging.

It is not a hype brand. It is not a performance brand. It is not a shame-based health product.

The emotional stance is simple:

- glad you showed up
- no one is grading your pace
- returning is part of the work
- be kind to yourself
- keep going

## Core brand lines

Primary:

- Glad You're Here.
- Keep Going.

Supporting lines:

- Be kind to yourself.
- Returning is the work.
- Showing up counts.
- The pace can change without the progress disappearing.

## Usage guidance

### Good contexts for “Glad You're Here. Keep Going.”

- welcome / entry states
- re-entry after a quiet stretch
- hard-week recovery surfaces
- empty states where the product wants to feel human and steady
- splash / launch moments if the app tone stays restrained

### Good contexts for “Be kind to yourself.”

- privacy screens
- inspire-me surfaces
- hard-week support copy
- moments where the product needs to lower pressure without lowering dignity

## Logo direction

The initial Outgrow logo mark should suggest:

- growth
- gentleness
- life
- steadiness
- heart without sentimentality
- leaf without generic wellness cliché

The provided SVG mark in `assets/logo/outgrow-mark.svg` is an initial placeholder / working asset, not a final locked brand mark.

## Visual guidance

- warm off-white backgrounds
- sage / moss / muted greens
- occasional clay / sand warmth
- generous spacing
- rounded forms
- minimal ornament
- reflective, not glossy

## Product reminder

Outgrow should look and sound like a place where a person can return without shame.
